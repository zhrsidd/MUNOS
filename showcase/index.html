THE WHOLE PICTURE
  1. 01 / CHAIR↗

    Review.
    Then send to Approval.

    Delegates register their Google Doc with the committee. The Chair reviews it in Google Docs, then submits the resolution to the Approval Panel.

    Next → Approval Panel review
  2. 02 / APPROVAL PANEL↗

    Give feedback.
    Approve what’s ready.

    The panel reviews the resolution and records its decision. Changes requested go back to the Chair; approved resolutions move forward to Printing.

    Next → Approved work to Printing
  3. 03 / PRINTING↗

    Print.
    Keep the room moving.

    The Printing team works from its queue of approved resolutions and marks copies as printed, ready for delivery to the committee Chair, keeping the next handoff clear.

    Next → Back to the committee for debate
↶

Feedback has a way back. Changes requested return to the Chair for revision and resubmission. Executive oversight stays available across the workflow.
