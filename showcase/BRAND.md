# MUNOS — connected diplomacy

Standalone showcase identity. This does not replace the DCMUN application design system in docs/BRAND.md.

## Colour roles

| Token | Colour | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UN-inspired brand field | #009EDB | Large solid panels and graphic accents |
| Reading ink | #062B42 | Headlines, body and small text over cyan |
| Marine | #0B3D5B | Structural dark surfaces |

| Ice | #E4F3FA | Oversight band, quiet emphasis |

| Muted ink | #426478 | Secondary text on white/ice |
| Rule | #C8DCE7 | Dividers and borders |

UN blue is a visual reference, not an affiliation. No UN emblem is used. The MUNOS mark is a simplified assembly column: three marine pillars, one lintel and a base, flanked by sparse cyan laurel branches. It replaces the old gradient badge and preserves the familiar column-and-laurel identity. The exact same vector geometry appears in the favicon, symbol, wordmark lockup and brand sheets. All colours are flat; no raster imagery or font dependency is embedded in the SVG assets.

## Composition

Diplomatic editorial design: generous white space, fine rules, nearly square corners, solid cyan fields and a technical line globe. No generated gradients, glass panels, glow or large blurred shadows. Fraunces display typography and Space Grotesk interface/body remain self-hosted. Scroll storytelling highlights a white active stage under a persistent Executive oversight band. Mobile and reduced-motion layouts present all content without pinning.

Small text never uses white on bright cyan. White is reserved for large display text there; small text uses reading ink. Action blue on white exceeds 4.5:1. Focus outlines use action blue on light surfaces and light cyan on dark surfaces.

## Logo assets

`public/munos-symbol.svg` is the icon. `public/munos-logo.svg` is the primary horizontal lockup with an outlined Space Grotesk wordmark. `public/favicon.svg` uses the same icon on white for reliable visibility in browser tabs. `public/munos-logo.png` is a transparent PNG export. `public/munos-brand-sheet.svg` and `.png` present the updated identity and palette. Keep the icon’s clear space; do not add gradients, rounded badges or extra laurel leaves.
